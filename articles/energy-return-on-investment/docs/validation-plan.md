# Validation plan

1. Reject negative energy inputs.
2. Reject zero total investment.
3. Validate basic EROI arithmetic.
4. Validate net energy and net-energy fractions.
5. Validate energy-payback calculations.
6. Validate storage-adjusted delivered EROI.
7. Confirm Monte Carlo repeatability for fixed seeds.
8. Confirm uncertainty quantiles are ordered.
9. Confirm all generated project EROI values are positive.
10. Confirm plant and delivered boundary cases are both exported.
11. Parse CSV and JSON outputs.
12. Confirm the generated SVG has an SVG root element.
