# Method notes

## EROI

EROI is calculated as gross lifetime energy returned divided by energy invested within the selected boundary.

## Net energy

Net energy is gross returned energy minus invested energy. The net-energy fraction is net energy divided by gross returned energy.

## Boundaries

Plant, lifecycle, delivered, and extended EROI are not interchangeable. Comparisons require harmonized output carriers and boundary definitions.

## Storage

Storage is treated as an energetic burden on delivered energy, not as a primary energy source. Round-trip efficiency and embodied energy both affect delivered EROI.

## Uncertainty

The companion uses deterministic Monte Carlo sampling with fixed seeds so results are reproducible.

## Scope

The synthetic examples are educational and should not be interpreted as measured EROI estimates for real technologies.
