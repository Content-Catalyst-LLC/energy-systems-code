# Method notes

## Accounting levels

Primary energy enters the accounting boundary as an unconverted resource or as a conventionally assigned equivalent. Secondary energy follows a conversion step. Final energy reaches the end user. Useful energy is delivered by the end-use device. Service-equivalent energy represents the portion associated with the defined service outcome.

## Losses and uses

The model separates energy-sector own use, non-energy use, transformation loss, delivery loss, end-use loss, and service loss. These categories must not be double counted.

## Renewable and nuclear primary energy

The direct-equivalent method counts non-combustible electricity at generated electricity value. The substitution method estimates the thermal primary energy that would have been required at a stated reference efficiency. Neither method is universally correct for every question; the selected convention must be reported.

## Site and source energy

Site energy is measured at the building or facility boundary. Source energy applies an upstream factor. Source factors vary by grid, fuel, geography, year, and institutional method.

## Scope limits

This package is an educational and diagnostic accounting model. It does not replace official national energy balances, plant dispatch models, lifecycle assessment, process simulation, or detailed service-demand models.
