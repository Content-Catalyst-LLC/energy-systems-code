# Validation plan

1. Confirm every efficiency and allocation fraction stays within its valid range.
2. Confirm own use plus non-energy use never exceeds primary energy.
3. Confirm each pathway conserves energy across all reported stages and losses.
4. Confirm aggregate residuals remain within numerical tolerance.
5. Confirm final energy does not exceed primary energy under the stated chain model.
6. Confirm useful energy does not exceed final energy.
7. Confirm service-equivalent energy does not exceed useful energy.
8. Confirm direct-equivalent and substitution methods remain separately labeled.
9. Confirm deterministic uncertainty results repeat for a fixed seed.
10. Cross-check baseline calculations in Python, R, Julia, C, and SQL when available.
11. Validate CSV, JSON, and SVG outputs before commit.
