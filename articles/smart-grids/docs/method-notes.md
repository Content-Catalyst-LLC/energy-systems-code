# Method notes

## Interval data

Hourly synthetic demand demonstrates peak identification and load factor.

## Managed charging

Charging power is total required energy divided by the available charging window. Real charging control must include connection time, charger limits, mobility requirements, feeder constraints, and customer preferences.

## FLISR

Customer interruption-hours avoided compare automated restoration time with a manual switching baseline.

## DER coordination

Expected dispatchable DER capacity multiplies nameplate power by availability and response fraction. Production programs need telemetry, location, network constraints, and performance verification.

## Communications

Parallel communications availability assumes independent failures for a simple teaching illustration.

## Scope

Smart-grid value depends on accurate network data, cyber-secure communications, operational integration, and the physical limits of the underlying grid.
