# Method notes

## Transmission

Three-phase current demonstrates why high voltage reduces current for a given real-power transfer.

## Losses

Resistive losses use the standard three-phase relationship 3 I^2 R.

## Distribution

Feeder and transformer utilization are screening measures based on apparent-power ratings.

## Hosting capacity

Synthetic cases represent added load and distributed generation relative to feeder capability. Real hosting-capacity studies also require voltage, protection, fault-current, time-series, and power-quality analysis.

## Expansion planning

The ranking model is deliberately transparent and illustrative. It combines capacity, cost, loss reduction, congestion savings, and implementation time; it is not a utility investment model.
