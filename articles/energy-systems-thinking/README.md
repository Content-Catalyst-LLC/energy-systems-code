# Energy Systems Thinking

Computational companion for Article 7 of the Sustainable Catalyst Energy Systems series.

Includes stocks and flows, capacity additions and retirements, learning curves, reinforcing and balancing feedback, transmission/manufacturing/permitting constraints, rebound analysis, scenario dynamics, deterministic uncertainty, and Python/R/Julia/C examples.

Run:

```bash
./run_all.sh
```

The scenarios are synthetic teaching models and are not forecasts.
