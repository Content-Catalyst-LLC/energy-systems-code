# Method notes

## Reliability indices

SAIDI, SAIFI, and CAIDI use sustained customer interruption counts and durations.

## Adequacy

Reserve margin is included as a deterministic screening metric. Monte Carlo capacity and load variation provide a simple probabilistic teaching example.

## EUE

Expected unserved energy weights outage magnitude and duration by scenario probability.

## Resilience

Annualized risk is probability multiplied by consequence. Restoration capability is represented using area under the service-restoration curve.

## Scope

Real reliability studies should incorporate event definitions, major-event-day treatment, weather, correlated failures, network constraints, storage chronology, fuel availability, and customer criticality.
